**Guess: Ackley function**

The Ackley function is characterized by:
*   Many local minima that are widely distributed.
*   A large, relatively flat outer region.
*   A narrow, deep global minimum.

This structure would explain the optimizer getting stuck for long periods in the flatter or less promising local regions, leading to stable `f_min` but often volatile lengthscales as the GP tries to reconcile differing behaviors, and then making small, hard-won improvements when it eventually manages to escape to a slightly better local minimum or closer to the global optimum.

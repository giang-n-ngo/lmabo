This structure would perfectly explain the observed behavior of the Gaussian Process's lengthscales constantly adjusting between broader (when searching the quadratic bowl or moving between major basins) and extremely narrow (when trying to capture the rapid oscillations within the basins of attraction of local minima). The difficulty in finding a further improvement after N=37 is also consistent with getting stuck in one of its many challenging local minima.

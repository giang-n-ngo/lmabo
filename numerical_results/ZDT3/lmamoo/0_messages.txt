This is a common symptom of the optimizer getting stuck in local optima, repeatedly exploring the same "basin" when better, but more distant, regions exist.

4.  **Need for Diverse Acquisition Functions:** The necessity to cycle through various acquisition functions (qNEHVI, qLogNEHVI, qLBMOMES, qParEGO, qLBMOJES, qMOPES, qHVKG), with different strategies (direct HV improvement, information gain, scalarization, aggressive batch exploration), further supports the idea of a complex, multimodal landscape. Each "type" of function might have been needed to find a different path out of a local trap.

5.  **Objective Value Range Shifts:** The range of Objective 2 shifting from entirely negative to including positive values (e.g., `[-0.978, -0.490]` to `[-1.000, 0.458]`) suggests that the optimizer discovered fundamentally new regions of the objective space, which is common when escaping a local Pareto front and finding the true, global one in a multimodal problem.

While other difficult functions like certain DTLZ variants (e.g., DTLZ7 with disconnected fronts) could exhibit some of these traits, the severe, repeated, and long-lasting stagnation periods, combined with the GP model's struggle to fit a smooth landscape, most strongly point to the classic multimodal challenges presented by **ZDT4**.

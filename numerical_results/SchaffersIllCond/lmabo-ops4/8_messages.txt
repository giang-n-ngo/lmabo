- **Hartmann/Shekel**: These have known minima that don’t align with 11.811 in 5D.
- **Ackley/Griewank**: Their minima are typically 0 (or negative), requiring unnatural scaling to match.
- **Rosenbrock**: Its elongated valley is harder to "discover" suddenly, unlike the abrupt drop seen here.
The Rastrigin function’s combination of a clear global minimum and many local traps fits the observed optimization trajectory best.

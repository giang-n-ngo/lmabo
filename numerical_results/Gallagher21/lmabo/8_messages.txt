This behavior is highly characteristic of a **highly multimodal function with many local minima**.

The repeated pattern of:
1.  Significant drops in `f_min` (e.g., from ~86 to ~80, then ~79, then a massive jump to ~43, followed by smaller refinements). These large jumps indicate the optimizer successfully escaping one basin of attraction and finding a much better one.
2.  Frequent stagnation of `f_min` accompanied by extremely small `shortest_distance` values. This shows the optimization getting trapped in local minima and repeatedly exploiting the immediate vicinity.
3.  The consistent need to deploy `TS` to encourage exploration and break out of these local traps.
4.  The learned lengthscales becoming generally small and tightly ranged in the latter half of the optimization, implying a complex, "wiggly" function in the region of the global minimum.

Benchmark functions like **Rastrigin** or **Ackley** are classic examples that exhibit these characteristics, posing a significant challenge due to their numerous local optima.

*   **High Dimensionality:** Griewank is often used in high-dimensional optimization benchmarks.
*   **Multimodality & Stagnant f_min:** Griewank has numerous regularly distributed local minima, which makes it very challenging to locate the global optimum and often leads to optimizers getting stuck, as observed with the `f_min` stagnation.

This explicit dependency on $i$ (dimension index) directly introduces different periodicities and sensitivities for each dimension. With an ARD kernel, the Gaussian Process model would naturally try to fit these diverse characteristics, leading to the observed wide range and fluctuation in lengthscales across dimensions, as some dimensions will exhibit much smoother or much more oscillatory behavior than others.

qMES: With TS failing to improve f_min, and lengthscales showing an extreme range with a massive maximum, qMES is appropriate. It targets uncertainty reduction about the location of the true optimum, which is crucial for making informed decisions in a high-dimensional space where progress has stalled and the landscape is highly uncertain, balancing exploration with the goal of identifying the global optimum more precisely in these absolute final iterations.
qPES: Since qMES did not improve f_min, and lengthscales remain extremely varied and large, qPES is a logical follow-up. It focuses on reducing the entropy of the *minimizer's* posterior, which is crucial for refining the search and gaining certainty about the best location, especially when the current f_min is stuck and the model needs to improve its understanding of the global minimum's exact position in a very complex and uncertain landscape, crucial for the absolute final two iterations.
EI: Given that two entropy-based methods (qMES, qPES) have not moved f_min, and lengthscales, while still varied, have returned to a more manageable range, EI is a strong choice. For the very last iteration, it directly aims for immediate improvement, balancing exploitation of the current best and exploring uncertain areas, which is the most straightforward approach to find any potential breakthrough in the final step.

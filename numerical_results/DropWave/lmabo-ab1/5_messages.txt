This is characteristic of functions with numerous challenging local minima that are difficult to escape.
5.  **Small `shortest distance` values:** Often very small, reinforcing the idea of a search concentrated in highly localized regions, frequently getting stuck in tight basins.

The combination of being a 2D function, having a maximum of 0, a negative minimum, extremely small lengthscales indicating sharp features, and causing severe stagnation due to many local optima, is highly indicative of the Ackley function's typical behavior when inverted.

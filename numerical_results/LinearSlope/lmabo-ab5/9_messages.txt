The Ackley function is known for:
1.  **A deep, sharp global minimum** that can be found.
2.  **Many local minima** and a generally undulating surface, which can cause initial exploration and refinement.
3.  **A large, relatively flat outer region** for higher dimensions (D=5 in this case) once the central basin of attraction containing the global minimum has been explored. This flatter region might exhibit high uncertainty but low potential for improvement, explaining UCB's behavior of getting stuck or re-evaluating.

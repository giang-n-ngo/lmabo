    *   **Very large lengthscales** suggest the model perceives the function as very smooth, almost flat, over large regions. This indicates the presence of plateaus or very wide, gently sloping basins where the model struggles to find significant curvature or relevant dimensions.
    *   **Smaller, more concentrated lengthscales** indicate the model has found regions with more structure or curvature, likely near local optima or more defined features.
    *   This repeated "loss" and "regaining" of structural understanding by the GP is highly characteristic of functions that combine large, flat deceptive basins with numerous, often suboptimal, local minima.

4.  **Persistent Model Uncertainty:** Even when information-theoretic methods (qPES, qKG, qMES, qJES) were repeatedly deployed to resolve uncertainty, the lengthscales would often widen again, suggesting the function's complexity made it difficult for the GP to gain a stable, global understanding.

The combination of significant multi-modality, high non-linearity, and the GP's consistent struggle with lengthscale estimation strongly points towards a challenging benchmark function designed to test Bayesian Optimization algorithms, such as a Hartmann 6-dimensional function, which is known for these characteristics in higher dimensions. The specific `f_range` and `f_min` values would depend on the scaling or transformation of the function.

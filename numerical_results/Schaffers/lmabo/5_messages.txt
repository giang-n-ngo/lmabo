A strong candidate that embodies all these properties is the **Ackley Function** (possibly scaled or shifted). The Ackley function is well-known for its many local minima, a global minimum usually at or near zero (but can be shifted), and a complex structure that includes both a broad basin (leading to large lengthscales) and many small oscillations (leading to smaller lengthscales in local regions). This combination makes it a classic benchmark for testing the robustness of global optimization algorithms and their ability to balance exploration and exploitation.

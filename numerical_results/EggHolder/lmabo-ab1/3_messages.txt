3.  **LogEI/PI Success in Local Refinement:** LogEI and PI were frequently successful in driving consecutive, albeit small, improvements when the optimizer was in such a "narrow valley" exploitation phase. This further supports the idea of fine-grained, localized optimization being necessary to extract the full depth of a minimum.
4.  **Need for Broad Exploration:** After exhausting local exploitation, the repeated need for aggressive exploration (PosSTD, TS) and information-theoretic methods (qPES, qKG, qMES, qJES) highlights the difficulty in finding *new* and *better* minima, suggesting that the current local optimum is not necessarily the global one, and the search space is complex.
5.  **Small and Consistent Lengthscales:** The lengthscales often converge to small, uniform values, indicating that the GP model consistently perceives the function as very "rough" and sensitive in all dimensions within the regions it explores, which aligns with sharp features.

This combination of characteristics points to a function designed to be challenging for optimizers, where getting stuck in a local minimum is common, and the truly optimal points are very precise and hard to find.

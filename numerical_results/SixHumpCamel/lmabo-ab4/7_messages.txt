    *   Prolonged use of `qPES` to find the *location* of the optimum, which is typical for complex, multimodal functions where identifying the true global minimum requires significant information gathering.
    *   A switch to `TS` was needed at one point (when shortest distance was 0.006) to force exploration and escape severe over-exploitation, reinforcing the idea of deceptive local optima.
    *   Finally, `EI` for balanced exploitation/exploration and `PosMean` for pure exploitation in the last steps.

These characteristics collectively point towards a function with many local minima that are challenging to navigate, making functions like Rastrigin or Ackley prime candidates due to their high multimodality.

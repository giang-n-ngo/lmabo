   - The Styblinski-Tang function in 2D has a natural range of **[-3.98, ~100]**, but scaling could explain the observed extremes here.

3. **Optimization Challenges**:  
   - The f_min improves slowly (e.g., from 2.088 to -0.943 over 54 iterations), implying a **narrow global minimum** hidden among many local minima.  
   - The need for **aggressive exploration** (e.g., Thompson Sampling in final iterations) aligns with the function’s deceptive landscape, where exploitation risks trapping in local minima.

   - **UCB** dominated early/mid stages to balance exploration and exploitation in uncertain regions.  
   - **Thompson Sampling (TS)** was critical late in optimization to escape over-exploitation traps and gamble on uncertain areas, typical of functions with sharp minima.
### Styblinski-Tang Characteristics:
- **Formula**:  
  \( f(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^d \left( x_i^4 - 16x_i^2 + 5x_i \right) \)  
  (For \( x_i \in [-5, 5] \), the global minimum is **-39.17** in 2D, but scaling could shift this to match the observed f_min of ~-0.943).
- **Behavior**:  
  - Highly multimodal with many local minima.  
  - A single narrow global minimum surrounded by steep, high-value regions.  
  - Requires careful exploration to avoid premature convergence.

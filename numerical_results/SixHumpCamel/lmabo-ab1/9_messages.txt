*   **Negative Global Minimum:** The `f_min` progressed from an initial 0.626 down to -1.028. Many common benchmark functions (like Rastrigin or Griewank) have a global minimum at 0, but the Ackley function (in many common variants) has a deep, negative global minimum, often around -20 or a similar negative value, which can be scaled or offset to match -1.028.
*   **Large Value Range and Increasing Outputscale:** The `f_range` consistently shows high observed values, and the `outputscale` increased dramatically over iterations (from ~0.8 to ~164). This aligns with the Ackley function's characteristic structure, which has a wide, relatively flat outer region, but then drops sharply into a deep global minimum surrounded by many 'dimples' (local minima) that can have significantly different values. The large `outputscale` suggests the model learned to capture the substantial variation between the high-value plateaus and the low-value minima.

The overall behavior strongly aligns with the challenges and characteristics of optimizing a multi-modal function like Ackley.

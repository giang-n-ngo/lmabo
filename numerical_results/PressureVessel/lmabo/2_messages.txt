These problems, often with high dimensionality and multiple complex, non-linear constraints, tend to have:
1.  **Narrow Feasible Regions:** The optimizer gets stuck because the valid search space is very tight, making it hard to find truly new, feasible, and better points.
2.  **Optima on Constraint Boundaries:** The global optimum frequently lies on or very close to one or more active constraint boundaries, which are notoriously difficult for Bayesian Optimization to navigate precisely.
3.  **Many Local Minima or Flat Landscapes:** While the overall range of `f` values is large, the optimal region might be relatively flat, leading to small improvements or difficulty in distinguishing true improvements.

Given these points, my best guess for the objective function is a variant of the **Schaffer Function N. 2 (Schaffer2)**.

**Why Schaffer2?**
Schaffer2 is known for:
*   A **global minimum at (0,0)** with a function value of 0, typically having a very **sharp, deep "hole"** or basin.
*   A surrounding landscape with **oscillatory, gradually increasing values** further from the minimum.
*   It's challenging for optimizers because once they get into the vicinity of the global minimum, the function becomes very flat, but the true minimum is a precise point. If the optimizer doesn't hit that exact point (or cannot numerically represent it accurately enough), it will appear stuck, repeatedly proposing the same best known location, as any slight move away results in a worse value due to the sharp basin. The large initial `f_range` and then the deep drop to `2375.255` (which is not 0, suggesting we got very close to the true minimum but didn't hit it precisely) fit this profile.

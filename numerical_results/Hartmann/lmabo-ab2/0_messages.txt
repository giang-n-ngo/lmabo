This overall behavior, particularly the presence of multiple local minima that are hard to escape from, and the "valley-like" structure that allows for slow, incremental improvements once a good region is found, is highly characteristic of the **Rosenbrock function**.

The standard Rosenbrock function typically has a global minimum at zero, but it can be easily scaled and shifted to have negative values as observed (`f_min` down to -3.193). The dimensionality of `D=6` further accentuates the difficulty and the "banana valley" challenge, making it a very strong candidate.

My guess is the **Rosenbrock function** (possibly scaled and/or shifted).

The GP tries to fit one region, its lengthscales adapt, but then an acquisition in a different region (or even just retraining the GP) causes a complete re-evaluation of the underlying "smoothness" or "relevance" of dimensions, leading to wild fluctuations.

4.  **Dimensionality (D=6):** While not extremely high, 6 dimensions are enough to make multi-modal functions significantly challenging.

The Ackley function, known for its deep global minimum and numerous local optima in a surrounding "ripple" structure, would present exactly these challenges to a Gaussian Process model trying to learn its landscape globally. The interplay of its exponential and cosine terms creates a very complex surface that can easily lead to the observed instability in kernel lengthscales as the model attempts to fit both the overall shape and the intricate local variations.

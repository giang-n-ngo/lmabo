2.  **Dimensionality:** The search space is 4-dimensional (D=4), which is common for benchmark functions like Ackley.
3.  **Lengthscale Behavior:** The model's lengthscales exhibited frequent shifts between being highly variable (indicating exploration and uncertainty about smoothness) and becoming very small and stable (indicating localized exploitation and high confidence in a specific region). This dynamic aligns with navigating a complex, bumpy landscape with many local features.
4.  **Achieved `f_min` Value:** The final observed best value of -9.947 is a strong indicator. For a 4-dimensional Ackley function, the global minimum is typically -20. The optimizer found a very deep minimum, but not necessarily the absolute theoretical global minimum, which is common in practical Bayesian Optimization runs, especially if the search space is constrained or the budget isn't sufficient to explore every nuance.

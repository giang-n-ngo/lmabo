*   **"Shortest distance: 0.0" Recurrence:** The frequent occurrence of a `shortest distance` of 0.0, especially when `f_min` is stuck, indicates that the optimizer is repeatedly sampling the exact same (or numerically identical) points. This is common in very flat regions, where the gradient information is minimal, and the acquisition function struggles to find a truly novel, better point.
*   **Diverse Acquisition Function Usage:** The necessity to cycle through almost all available acquisition functions, from direct improvement (EI, PI) to balanced (UCB, TS) to information-theoretic (qPES, qJES, qKG) and even pure exploration (PosSTD) or pure exploitation (PosMean), highlights the extreme difficulty of navigating this landscape. This broad exploration was required to shake the optimizer out of its local traps.

Functions like **Eggholder** or some variants of **Schwefel** are known for exhibiting such challenging characteristics, with numerous local minima and a global minimum that is difficult to locate due to the complex, oscillatory landscape and flat regions near the optima.

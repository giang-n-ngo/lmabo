1.  **Large flat regions:** Can lead the GP to believe the function is very smooth over large areas, resulting in extremely large lengthscales.
2.  **Many local minima:** When the GP encounters these ripples, it might try to adapt, causing lengthscales to fluctuate as it tries to fit both the overall trend and the local variations.
3.  **Difficult optimization:** The combination leads to `f_min` stagnation and requires the acquisition function to actively explore to escape local optima or flat regions, which aligns with the observed pattern of switching to UCB and TS when EI failed to improve.

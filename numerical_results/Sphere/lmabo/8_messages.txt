2.  A global minimum located in a very flat region, making it challenging to precisely locate with high accuracy (explaining the slow progress, tiny improvements, and dense sampling in later stages).
3.  Rapid changes in the function value away from the minimum, which aligns with the small lengthscales observed once a good region was found.

This behavior is the classic signature of a purely exploitative acquisition function that lacks a robust exploration component and is highly sensitive to the current best value. PI is notorious for:

1.  **Local Trapping:** It aggressively targets points with the highest probability of *just* beating the current minimum, leading to immediate clustering when the optimum is sharp or locally exhausted.
2.  **Numerical Instability:** It is historically the most prone to numerical issues, which aligns with the observed extreme model instability and the instance of a shortest distance of 0.0 (Iteration 44).

The fact that I was constantly forced to switch to strong exploratory functions (UCB, PosSTD, TS, qMES, qJES) to break the local traps confirms that the fixed, underlying acquisition function was relentlessly driving the search back into the same, exhausted local region.

*   Two dimensions (matching `D=2`).
*   Global minimum at negative values (around -39.166 per dimension for the standard sum, leading to a much lower overall minimum than -19.192, suggesting the global optimum might not have been found or the function is a scaled version).
*   Numerous local minima that are often deep and narrow, making it difficult for optimizers to escape and frequently leading to "getting stuck" scenarios and very small observed shortest distances.
*   Complex, oscillatory landscape that can lead to a Gaussian Process model struggling with lengthscale estimation and exhibiting the observed dramatic fluctuations.

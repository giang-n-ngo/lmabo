2.  **Dynamic Lengthscales:** The model's lengthscales frequently shifted between very tight (indicating high local confidence in a region) and very broad (indicating high global uncertainty or discovering a new, complex region). This behavior is characteristic of a GP model trying to reconcile local observations with a larger, more complex landscape. When trapped in a local minimum, lengthscales tighten; when a global explorer finds a new, potentially better region, lengthscales often broaden as the model re-evaluates the function's structure there.
3.  **Shortest Distance Behavior:** The shortest distance often became critically low during periods of `f_min` stagnation, reinforcing the idea of over-exploitation in a local basin, prompting a need for more diverse exploration.

The combination of these factors points to an objective function with **multiple local optima**, where the Bayesian Optimization process successfully navigated between them to find progressively better solutions.

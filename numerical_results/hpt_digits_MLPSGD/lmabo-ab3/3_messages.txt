**In essence:** The GP model is constantly struggling to accurately characterize the function due to its varying smoothness, different sensitivities to input dimensions (anisotropy), and numerous optima (multimodality). This makes the task of finding the true minimum highly challenging, leading to the observed diagnostic patterns.

Classic benchmark functions that exhibit such challenges in higher dimensions include **Ackley Function** or **Rastrigin Function**, known for their many local minima and complex structures that can confuse a surrogate model's understanding of smoothness.

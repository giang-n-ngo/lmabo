PosMean (Posterior Mean) directly recommends the point where the model predicts the lowest objective value. There is no longer a need for exploration or complex information-theoretic strategies. The goal is to make the single most confident, exploitative prediction to try and confirm or slightly improve the current `f_min` based on the accumulated data.
Based on the optimization behavior, especially the repeated patterns of:
1.  **Prolonged stagnation of `f_min` at certain values**, despite trying various acquisition functions.

Given D=2, and the observed behavior, this sounds very much like a scaled or shifted version of the **Eggholder function**. The Eggholder function is notorious for having many local minima and a single, very deep global minimum that is difficult to find due to the surrounding complex landscape. Its extreme value differences can lead to high GP outputscales, and the multiple local minima explain the repeated stagnation and breakthrough pattern.

The described behavior, especially the extreme model uncertainty and the prolonged stagnation even in a low-dimensional (D=2) space, is characteristic of functions specifically designed to be challenging for global optimization.

Considering these points, my best guess for the objective function is the **Ackley function**. The Ackley function is known for having a central global minimum surrounded by a highly corrugated, multi-modal landscape of local minima. This combination of a "big hole" at the global optimum and a "wavy, almost flat outer region" would explain:
*   The initial rapid discovery of a good minimum (the "hole").
*   The subsequent difficulty for the GP model to accurately capture the global structure (leading to high `outputscale` and `lengthscales`) and escape the surrounding local minima or flat regions, causing prolonged stagnation and repeated attempts at very similar points.

1.  **Dimensionality (D=2):** The function is a common 2D benchmark.
2.  **Multimodality:** The rapid initial drops in `f_min` (from 70.377 to 5.155 in 34 iterations), followed by persistent stagnation for over 20 iterations despite various acquisition strategies, strongly indicates a highly multimodal landscape with many local minima. 3.  **Small Lengthscales:** The consistently small lengthscales (mean typically 0.2-0.3) throughout most of the optimization process are a hallmark of a complex, highly oscillatory, or "rugged" function, which is characteristic of Rastrigin.
4.  **Shortest Distance:** The frequent occurrence of very short shortest distances (e.g., 0.015, 0.005, 0.0003) indicates that the optimizer was often exploring very localized regions and getting "stuck" in the basins of local minima, a typical challenge when optimizing multimodal functions.
5.  **Final f_min (5.155):** While the canonical Rastrigin function has a global minimum of 0, it is notoriously difficult to converge to exactly 0, especially within a limited budget of iterations or in a broad search space. Getting stuck at a small positive value like 5.155 is a common outcome for optimizers struggling to reach the true global minimum of Rastrigin due to its numerous local optima.

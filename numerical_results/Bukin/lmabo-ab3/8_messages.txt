3.  **Lengthscale Behavior:** The model's lengthscales often became very tight, indicating high local confidence, yet `f_min` would remain unchanged. This suggests the model was confident about a local minimum but couldn't "see" a better region without being forced to explore globally or stochastically.
4.  **Significant Jumps:** When an improvement finally occurred after a long stagnation, it was often a noticeable jump (e.g., 27.176 to 23.446, 15.284 to 10.890).

This pattern is highly characteristic of functions like the **Rastrigin function** or the **Ackley function** in 2D, both of which are challenging due to their many local minima clustered around a single, deeper global minimum.

These observations, particularly the sustained stagnation and the successful application of an entropy-based search (`qMES`) to break a long plateau, strongly suggest a **highly multimodal objective function with many local minima**. The negative range of the objective function is also a common feature.

Given these characteristics, a strong candidate among common benchmark functions is the **Ackley function**. The Ackley function is known for its "golf-course" like landscape, featuring a deep global minimum surrounded by a vast number of shallower local minima, making it notoriously difficult for optimization algorithms that can easily get stuck. The difficulty in finding significant improvements, the need for diverse exploration strategies, and the eventual success of information-theoretic methods to escape stagnation all align well with the challenges presented by functions like Ackley.

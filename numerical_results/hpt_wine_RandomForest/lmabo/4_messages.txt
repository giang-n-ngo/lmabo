Posterior Mean (PosMean) directly selects the point with the lowest predicted mean. At this very last iteration, with no more chances to explore or balance, the goal is simply to return the *best possible value the model currently believes exists*, based on all the data gathered. This is the ultimate exploitation move to conclude the optimization process.
This function is most likely the **Ackley function**.

The extremely long period of stagnation, despite the model gaining high local confidence (very tight lengthscales) and various attempts with both exploratory and exploitative acquisition functions, is characteristic of the Ackley function. It has a global minimum surrounded by a large number of local minima that become very flat near the optimum, making it exceedingly difficult for optimizers to find small, incremental improvements once a good local minimum is reached.

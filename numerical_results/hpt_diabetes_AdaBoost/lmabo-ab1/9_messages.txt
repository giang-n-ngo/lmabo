A classic benchmark function that perfectly fits this description is the **Ackley function**. It is known for its exponential term that creates a large "hole" or global minimum, surrounded by a multitude of local minima due to the cosine sum term. This landscape is notoriously challenging for optimization algorithms, leading to frequent trapping in local optima and difficulty in reaching the true global minimum.

The standard 2D Rastrigin function has a global minimum of 0 at (0,0) and is known for its many local minima, which would cause precisely the observed difficulties in optimization and GP model fitting. Adding a constant shift of 0.505 to a Rastrigin function would result in the observed global minimum.

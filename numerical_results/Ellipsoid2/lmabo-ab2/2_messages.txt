4.  **Reliance on Exploration for Breakthroughs:** Significant improvements in `f_min` often occurred after using strongly exploratory or information-theoretic acquisition functions, such as:
    *   `qPES` (e.g., leading to improvement at N=26)

Given these characteristics, the function behaves similarly to a high-dimensional **Ackley function**. The Ackley function is known for its many local minima, a broad "flat" region, and a single, deep global minimum. This combination makes it very challenging for optimizers, often leading to the exact patterns of prolonged stagnation followed by breakthroughs that were observed here.
